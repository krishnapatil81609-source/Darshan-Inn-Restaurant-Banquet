ABOUT DARSHAN INN

Pure Vegetarian Dining for
Families & Celebrations

Darshan Inn Restaurant is a dedicated pure vegetarian dining destination located on the 1st floor of Mark Point along Kharvasa-Dindoli Road in Dindoli, Surat.

Our premises include a spacious, air-conditioned family hall and banquet facility, thoughtfully designed for peaceful everyday family meals, weekend dinners, anniversaries, and community gatherings.

We focus on fresh preparation, generous portions, and authentic tastes across Punjabi specialties, tandoori breads, Indo-Chinese favorites, and evening snacks.
